FAQ

Pricing questions

Why don't you show fixed prices?

Because every business has different needs. A 3‑page website and a 50‑product store are very different projects. After a quick chat we send a clear, fixed quote — no hidden costs.

What are your payment terms?

One‑time projects: 50% advance, 50% on completion. Monthly services: billed monthly in advance, cancel anytime with notice.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer, JazzCash, Easypaisa and international methods like Wise/Payoneer for overseas clients.

Can I combine services from different packages?

Absolutely. All packages are flexible — mix and match the services you need and we'll quote accordingly.

Is ad spend included in marketing packages?

No. Ad budgets are paid directly to Google, Meta or TikTok from your own account for full transparency.
