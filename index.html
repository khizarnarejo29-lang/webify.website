FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How long does a website take?

A standard business website usually takes 5–10 working days. Larger e‑commerce or custom projects take longer — we'll give you a clear timeline before we start.

How many logo concepts do I get?

You'll receive multiple unique concepts to choose from, plus revisions on your favourite until it's perfect. Final files include PNG, JPG, SVG and PDF.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes! We work with clients worldwide. Everything is handled online through WhatsApp, email and video calls.

How soon will I see SEO results?

SEO is a long‑term investment. Most clients see noticeable ranking improvements within 2–4 months, depending on competition and keywords.

What is the payment process?

Usually 50% advance to start and the remaining 50% on completion. For monthly services like social media and SEO, billing is monthly.
