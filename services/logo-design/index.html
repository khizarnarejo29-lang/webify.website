FAQ

Logo Design questions

How long does logo design take?

You'll receive your first concepts within 2–4 working days. The full process, including revisions, usually takes about a week.

Will my logo be 100% original?

Yes. Every logo is designed from scratch by our designers. We never use templates or stock clip‑art, so you can trademark it.

What files will I receive?

AI, EPS, SVG, PDF, PNG (transparent) and JPG — in color, black and white versions — ready for print, web and social media.

Do I own the copyright?

Yes, once the project is complete you own 100% of the rights to your logo.

What if I don't like the concepts?

We'll go back to the drawing board with your feedback. Our goal is a logo you're genuinely proud of.
